Take A Moment

Take A Moment is a website built for those who have a need to express
themselves in written form. We as humans are creative beings but often
don't have an outlet to express that. So this is me attempting to battle that issue.
Here, you can put your thoughts into words without the fear of impressing
anyone but yourself. So come and take a moment to open your mind and write your story.
